### 1
A Confrontation-style war over the east half of the map. Boscali fly from a carrier group south of Fenwick and from the Fenwick and Kittiwake landing zones; Primeva fly from Graylock Strip, Graylock South and Brekka Hills. Aircraft factories west of Fenwick and on the Graylock peninsula build new airframes. The Ostrin Isles and Skerry Island start neutral; helicopter troops take them. Destroy the enemy aircraft factories to win. Everyone starts at rank 0.

### 2
A Primeva amphibious fleet is steaming through Ansel Reach to seize Port Ansel, with a second wave coming up Gannet Sound later. Boscali must sink the fleets before they burn the port's fuel tanks; Primeva win if the tank farm burns. AI aircraft fly for both sides.

### 3

Boscali must sink the landing force before its troops land and burn the Rook Island station.

### 4
A combined-arms war across the Cinder Range, built like the stock Confrontation. Each side has a main airbase, a forward helicopter base with a vehicle depot and three vehicle factories, and an aircraft factory complex. Factories keep both sides supplied with airframes and armour, and the ground war fights over two neutral landing zones in the middle. Break the enemy forward depot, then destroy their aircraft factories to win. Everyone starts at rank 0 with little money; nuclear weapons clear at escalation 256. Graylock Strip is closed.

### 5
A helicopter-heavy fight for the islands, in the spirit of Terminal Control. Boscali start at Sorrel Field, Rook Island and Kittiwake; Primeva at Graylock Strip, Graylock South and Brekka Hills. Fenwick (well defended), the Ostrin Isles and Skerry Island start neutral. Hold all three at once, or take the enemy's forward base, to win. Fenwick sits about 15 km from Kittiwake and 20 km from Graylock South.

### 6
A small, Altercation-size strike up Graylock Fjord. A Boscali carrier group sits at the fjord mouth with a forward pad on Skerry Island. Primeva hold the fjord with corvettes, a SAM site and armour guarding the supply depot at the Sill, the narrow crossing at the fjord head. Boscali must destroy the depot and its guard; Primeva win if they hold out for 45 minutes or sink the carrier group.

### 7
Air race on Ironjaw Sound: Fjord Sprint. Short, fast and low over water: north up Halvard Sound, through Ironjaw Narrows, west along Tarn Bay, a hop over the neck into the narrow north fjord and out through its S-bend to the open sea. 41 km, 26 gates, about 3.0 min in a fast jet. Any aircraft. Needs the TerrainLabRace plugin on the host: spawn at Sorrel Field and the host puts you on the start grid in the air. Host chat: !race grid (open the grid), !race go (start now), !race reset, !race status.

### 8
Air race on Ironjaw Sound: Glacier Run. Longer and technical: from Halvard's inner bay west up the Lower Ice, over The Col at about 800 m, down onto the Upper Ice, round into the long north valley and out over Tarn Head glacier to the strip. 41 km, 22 gates, about 3.0 min in a fast jet. Any aircraft. Needs the TerrainLabRace plugin on the host: spawn at Sorrel Field and the host puts you on the start grid in the air. Host chat: !race grid (open the grid), !race go (start now), !race reset, !race status.

### 9

The whole map: out from Sorrel Field, up the Delta river, north through Hollin Gap between the coast range and the high peaks, along Tarn Bay and the Narrows, south down Halvard Sound and home past Fenwick Island and Ansel Reach. 128 km, 43 gates, about 9.3 min in a fast jet. Any aircraft. Needs the TerrainLabRace plugin on the host: spawn at Sorrel Field and the host puts you on the start grid in the air. ### 11
Fjord Confrontation with a supply war. Each side has a supply yard at its forward helicopter base (Delta Gate, Cinder Gate) with crates of munitions and fuel, and a convoy brings three more crates every eight minutes for forty minutes. Sling crates under a UH-90 Ibis or VL-49 Tarantula and carry them to the front: a crate on the ground counts like a squad of troops toward capturing a site, and ground units can rearm and refuel from it. Take a landing zone, then build its supply dump by bringing crates to the concrete pad: a built dump gives a forward vehicle depot, an ammunition bunker, a munitions truck, a repair dozer and air defence, and raises your faction income. Break the enemy forward depot, then destroy their aircraft factories to win. Everyone starts at rank 0; nuclear weapons clear at escalation 256. Graylock Strip is closed.
